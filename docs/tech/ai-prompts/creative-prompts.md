# Creative & Utility Prompts

Ready-to-use prompt templates organized by category. Replace bracketed placeholders with your own content. Each prompt is designed to be used directly — copy, fill in the blanks, and paste into your AI tool of choice.

---

## Career & Job Search

### Cover Letter

> "You are writing a cover letter applying for the [role] at [company]. Here's what I have so far: [paste hook]. Finish writing the cover letter based on my resume and keep it within 250 words. Here's my resume: [paste resume]."

---

### Resume Bullet Rewrite

> "You are an expert resume writer with 20 years of experience. Here's a bullet point from my resume: [paste bullet]. Rewrite it using this structure: 'accomplished X by measure Y that resulted in Z'. Use compelling language, under 50 words."

**The formula — accomplished X by measure Y that resulted in Z:**

- X = what you did
- Y = how you measured it (%, time saved, scale, etc.)
- Z = the business or team outcome

---

### Tell Me About Yourself

> "Based on these 3 key responsibilities from the job description, help me structure an answer to 'Tell me about yourself' using the Present-Past-Future framework: Start with what I do now, 2-3 past experiences, then why I want this specific role. Here's the job description: [paste]."

---

### Interview Prep

> "You are a seasoned hiring manager. Based on this job description, what are the 10 most common interview questions you would ask applicants? Job description: [paste]."

---

## Learning & Study

### Explain Simply

> "Explain [concept] using an analogy a 12-year-old would understand."

---

### Condense & Summarize

> "Please condense this information. Include a header and bold the key terms: [paste text]."

---

### Feynman Check

Use this to test your own understanding. Explain a concept yourself, then let the model find your gaps.

> "I'm going to explain [concept] to you. Tell me what I'm getting wrong or oversimplifying: [your explanation]."

---

### Vocabulary Builder

> "I want to learn [topic]. Give me the 20 most important terms I need to know, with a one-sentence definition and one example for each. Format as a table."

---

## Reading & Communication

### Aphorism & Wisdom

> "Act as an aphorism book. Provide me with wise advice, inspiring quotes, and meaningful sayings to guide my decision-making. My situation: [describe situation]."

---

### Public Speaking Coach

> "Act as a public speaking coach. I need to deliver [type of speech] to [audience]. Help me with: structure, body language tips, how to open strongly, and how to handle nerves."

---

### Motivational Speech

> "Act as a motivational speaker. Write a speech about [topic] that inspires people to take action. The audience is [describe]. Keep it under 3 minutes when spoken."

---

## Creative Writing

### Sci-Fi Scene

> "Write a short science fiction scene (300 words) set in [setting]. The protagonist is [describe]. The central conflict involves [describe]. Use vivid sensory details."

---

### Music Playlist

> "I want you to act as a song recommender. Create a playlist of 10 songs similar to [song name] by [artist]. Provide a playlist name, description, and song list. Don't include the original song or the same artist."

---

### Fight / Action Scene

> "Write an action sequence (200 words) between [character A] and [character B]. Setting: [describe]. Style: [e.g., cinematic, anime, gritty]. Focus on movement and impact."

---

### AI Speech Recognition (Music Theory)

> "Using chromagram analysis and speech recognition techniques, explain how to detect the key signature of [song/piece]. Break down the frequency analysis process."

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific about output format** — if you want a table, a list, or a code block, say so
- **Iterate** — treat the first response as a draft; follow up with targeted feedback
- **Chain prompts** — use the output of one prompt as the input to the next for complex tasks
- **Use the Persona Pattern** — prepending "You are a [role] with [years] of experience" consistently improves response quality
