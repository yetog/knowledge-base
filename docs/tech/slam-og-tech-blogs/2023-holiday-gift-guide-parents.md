# 2023 Holiday Gift Guide: PARENTS

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/December-2023/2023-Holiday-Gift-Guide-PARENTS  
**Date:** December 2023  
**Author:** Rachelle Leath & Patrick Dieudonne

---

## Overview

This holiday gift guide focuses on present suggestions for parents, including both fur parents and traditional moms and dads. The curated selection emphasizes items that delight children while simplifying parental life.

## Featured Gift Recommendations

### Ashleigh Bakes Daily Cookies ($Varies)

Patrick, WTE's resident food enthusiast, endorses this bakery, stating "Cookies make everything better" and praising their quality.

### Solo Stove Ranger 2.0 Smokeless Fire Pit ($264.99)

An outdoor gathering solution enabling family bonding through marshmallow roasting and creating memories around controlled flames.

### Furbo Dog Camera ($69.30)

A pet monitoring device featuring treat-dispensing capability and video documentation of pets' activities for concerned owners.

### Anker SOLIX C1000 Portable Power Station ($699.00)

An off-grid power solution for outdoor enthusiasts seeking extended device charging during adventures.

### Tyfur Dome ($499.00)

Described as a "captivating centerpiece" that enhances holiday decoration aesthetics.

### K-Duo Special Edition Coffee Maker ($199.99)

Targets busy parents with dual brewing flexibility for individual servings or larger quantities.

### Surround Sound Shower System ($95.99)

Positioned as a relaxation sanctuary providing respite from parenting demands.
