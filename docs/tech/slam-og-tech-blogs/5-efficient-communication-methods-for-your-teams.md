# 5 Efficient Communication Methods for Your Teams

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/5-Efficient-Communication-Methods-for-Your-Teams  
**Date:** March 2022  
**Author:** Patrick

---

Communication serves as a foundational element for organizational success across nonprofits, clubs, businesses, and religious groups. Internal team connectivity proves especially critical in remote work environments. WTE recommends several communication methods:

## Office 365

Microsoft's cloud-based Office 365 provides current software versions through subscription. The service includes email accounts on custom domains, Teams, Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, and online storage options. "Most of the apps are available as online OR desktop versions, too." WTE functions as an authorized Microsoft reseller.

## Social Media

Platforms including Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter, and TikTok offer effective audience reach. Success requires strategic channel selection matching target demographics and consistent engagement.

## Email Marketing

Email remains valuable for customer outreach regarding deals and community opportunities. Organizations should use dedicated email marketing systems to protect domain reputation. WTE resells AgileMail Pro for this purpose.

## Slack

"Slack is a communications tool designed to help with a group's internal communication." It features instant messaging channels, video calling with screen sharing, and integrations with OneDrive, Gmail, and Teams. Free versions suit smaller organizations.

## Band

Operating similarly to Facebook newsfeeds, Band offers calendars, event reminders, location mapping, RSVPs, and multimedia sharing. Being free makes it suitable for nonprofits and community organizations.
