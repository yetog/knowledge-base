# Adobe's Backlash: The AI Third Rail

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/June-2024/Adobe-s-Backlash-The-AI-Third-Rail  
**Date:** June 2024  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

The article explores the controversy surrounding Adobe's AI integration efforts. The company has become a focal point in broader discussions about artificial intelligence's role in creative industries. The authors note their team uses AI daily but recognize the "fear, uncertainty, and doubt (FUD) AI creates," describing this as "AI's third rail."

## The Controversial Terms and Conditions Change

Adobe updated its terms to allow user-generated content for AI training purposes. While intended to enhance creative tools, many users — particularly creatives — viewed this as overreach and feared their work could be exploited without consent or compensation.

## The Creative Community's Strong Response

Photographers, designers, and artists reacted with anger and betrayal. Social media discussions centered on concerns that Adobe's AI could copy artistic expressions, generate derivative works without attribution, and diminish human creativity's value.

## The Ethical Dilemma

The core tension: AI promises to automate repetitive tasks but risks devaluing human creativity. Key questions emerged about ownership of creative works and the morality of AI mimicking human artistry.

## Stealing Our Souls

The phrase captures fears about losing authenticity in creative expression. For artists, work represents personal emotions and experiences — the concern is AI could produce art devoid of genuine human experience.

## Adobe's Failed Recovery

Adobe's clarifications and reassurances backfired. The company emphasized user content ownership and strict privacy guidelines, but trust was already damaged. The response exemplified the Streisand effect.

## Broader Implications

Adobe's situation reflects industry-wide struggles balancing AI's potential benefits against protecting human interests and values. The authors emphasize that "technology must be implemented carefully" with ethical considerations.

## Conclusion

The backlash underscores tension between innovation and creative integrity. Companies must navigate transparently while respecting users, maintaining ongoing dialogue about policies that honor human creativity.
