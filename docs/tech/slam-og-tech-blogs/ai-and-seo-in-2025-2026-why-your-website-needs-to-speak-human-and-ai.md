# AI and SEO in 2025/2026: Why Your Website Needs to Speak Human (and AI)

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/October-2025/AI-and-SEO-in-2025-2026-Why-Your-Website-Needs-to-Speak-Human-(and-AI)  
**Date:** October 2025  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Opening

The article establishes that search behavior has fundamentally shifted. As the author notes, "AI now anticipates audience questions. If your site can't answer those questions, it will be overlooked."

## Why Old-School SEO is Falling Behind

Traditional keyword-focused SEO tactics no longer suffice. Search engines now function as AI evaluators assessing relevance, authority, and content-user intent alignment. The piece cites a client example where traffic dropped 40 percent overnight because their content misaligned with user intent and AI interpretation.

## Start with Your Audience, Not Platforms

Focus should shift from platform optimization to addressing genuine user needs. The Texas Apartment Hunter case study illustrates this: renters seek utilities information, insurance details, local services, commuting options, and restaurant recommendations—not just listings. Content must guide potential customers across their entire decision journey.

## Entities Matter More Than Keywords

Modern ranking prioritizes entities and their relationships over isolated keywords. Consistency across touchpoints (LinkedIn, Crunchbase, YouTube, websites) builds authority and visibility.

## Tailor Content for Each Platform

- **YouTube:** Long-form trust-building videos
- **TikTok:** Short, attention-grabbing hooks
- **LinkedIn:** B2B insights and commentary
- **Reddit:** Direct question-answering assistance
- **Crunchbase:** Business facts and metrics for investors

## Technical Basics Still Matter

Page speed, metadata, JSON-LD, mobile-first design, natural phrasing for voice queries, and readable content remain essential.

## Test, Learn, Repeat

Track AI visibility, measure engagement, and iterate based on data. Engagement signals and transparency about data usage strengthen AI visibility.

## Key Takeaways

- Prioritize audience intent
- Build consistent entity graphs across touchpoints
- Master select platforms before expanding
- Publish regular pillar content with thoughtful repurposing
- Continuously test and measure

## Closing Message

Organizations must prepare now to communicate naturally to both human and AI audiences. The article concludes: "Be bold. Be trustworthy. Be human."
