# AI & Mobile First: Redefining Web Design

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/December-2023/AI-Mobile-First-Redefining-Web-Design  
**Date:** December 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

Mobile-first design and artificial intelligence are fundamentally reshaping how organizations approach web design, content creation, marketing, and SEO. As AI tools like chatbots become more sophisticated, they can identify connections that human marketers might overlook.

The author argues that content strategists should transition from "lone writer to intelligent curator." Rather than creating static, isolated articles, content should be developed as "modular, multifaceted components" that can be algorithmically recombined.

**Example Given:**

A post about Web3 technologies and one about the Lego-Fortnite collaboration may seem unrelated. However, an AI content management system could recognize that both involve virtual worlds using blockchain elements and suggest creating a fresh perspective piece combining insights from multiple sources.

## Our Lego Block Future

This approach enables continuous renewal of evergreen content through machine learning that identifies trending intersections. Rather than content becoming stale over time, algorithmic recombination keeps material relevant and aligned with real-time search patterns — offering "a new spin on SEO freshness."

The human element remains essential: identifying messages that genuinely resonate with audiences. Automation handles routine tasks, freeing strategists to focus on meaningful audience connections.

## Conclusion

The integration of human creativity with AI capabilities creates a "growth engine" transforming content marketing from speculation into strategic success. The future demands content curators prepared to partner with artificial intelligence.
