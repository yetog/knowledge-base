# AIvolution: How the AI Revolution is Changing Your World

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/April-2023/AIvolution-How-the-AI-Revolution-is-Changing-Your-World  
**Date:** April 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

Garrison opens by noting that revolutions often go unnoticed while happening. WTE Solutions has utilized AI tools like GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Azure for years without recognizing the revolutionary moment occurring around them.

## ChatGPT as a Turning Point

The piece identifies ChatGPT as the "killer app" that accelerated AI adoption dramatically. OpenAI's chatbot reached its early majority in just 2.5 months—far faster than Facebook's 10-month trajectory. This represents what Garrison calls "the fourth industrial revolution," with implications spanning healthcare, business models, and automation.

## Key Themes Covered

**Website Development:** ChatGPT will transform web design, FAQs, and user experience expectations. The technology enables more human-like interactions that users now anticipate.

**Developer Productivity:** GitHub Copilot and emerging tools like Copilot X significantly improve code completion and development speed. Garrison states these tools are "one of our most important development tools."

**Marketing & Branding:** AI will reshape digital marketing through personalized recommendations, enhanced SEO, predictive analytics, and CRM automation. The shift moves away from broadcast marketing toward individualized customer experiences.

**The AI Tsunami:** Rapid adoption will force businesses to adopt AI-powered solutions or risk appearing outdated through traditional templates.
