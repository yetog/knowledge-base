# Consumer Electronics Show Product Reviews

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Consumer-Electronics-Show-Product-Reviews  
**Date:** February 2022  
**Author:** Spence Wright

---

The WTE team attended CES 2022 virtually and reviewed home automation products that impressed them. They emphasize that free tickets and promotional items don't influence their evaluations.

## Top Picks

**Masonite Smart Door**
A full-frame door replacement featuring Yale smart deadbolt and Ring doorbell integration, powered by the door frame itself. Likely intended for contractors rather than DIY consumers.

**TP-LINK Archer AX10000 Tri Band WiFi 6 Router**
Supports both traditional 5GHz and newer 6GHz bands with impressive speed capabilities. Priced at approximately $287, though the team recommends waiting for firmware updates.

**Timberline Solar Shingles by GAF**
Low-profile solar shingles priced at roughly 50% less than competing options.

**BELKIN WEMO Doorbell**
Sleeker alternative to Ring, compatible with SmartThings and Apple HomeKit.

**Roborock S7 MaxV Ultra**
Advanced robotic vacuum and mop at $1,400 with built-in camera, lidar, and pet detection.

**Smart Slyder**
Motorized solution for sliding windows and doors.

**Govee RGB Light Panels**
"OLED type" lighting strips for behind-TV ambiance.

**Sengled Health Sensing Smart Bulb**
Uses radar to monitor heart rate, though WTE expresses privacy concerns.

**Air Quality Monitors**
Amazon's Alexa-integrated air monitor and emerging smart purifiers including ViraWarn.

## Weird Picks

- **Kohler's Suspen:** Ceiling-mounted kitchen faucet at $6,000
- **Rainstick:** WiFi shower recycling water ($3,500)
- **Qoobo Animated Pets:** Motion-responsive tail cushion

## Honorable Mentions

- TCL NXT Wear Air (affordable smart glasses)
- AnkerWorks B600 (video conferencing camera)
- iBabi Smart (AI baby monitor)
