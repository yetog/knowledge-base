# Content Marketing: Trends for 2023 and Beyond

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/June-2023/Content-Marketing-Trends-for-2023-and-Beyond  
**Date:** June 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Overview

The article emphasizes that content marketing success depends on blending emotional and factual elements. Garrison highlights key trends reshaping digital marketing strategies and the importance of understanding audience pain points to create targeted, effective content rather than infinite volume.

## Influencers, SEO, and Social Media

### Influencer Collaborations

Brands should partner with niche experts and micro-influencers to authentically reach target audiences. "Successful collaborations require careful selection of influencers aligned with your company and brand values" and integration into broader marketing strategies.

### SEO and Voice Search

High-quality content earns backlinks through case studies and webinars. As voice search grows, brands must optimize for conversational, long-tail queries and implement schema markup for smart devices like Alexa and Google Assistant.

### Social Media and Online Community

Platforms remain vital for distribution, with emphasis on shareable content. Building communities through Facebook and LinkedIn Groups fosters loyalty and encourages user-generated contributions.

## User Experience, Videos, UGC, Whitepapers

### Interactive Content

Quizzes, polls, and augmented reality experiences increase engagement and time on-site while boosting social sharing.

### Video Content

Short-form videos dominate due to smartphone penetration and limited attention spans. Platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts are key distribution channels.

### User-Generated Content

UGC provides authentic perspectives, builds community, and creates brand loyalty while reducing production demands on marketing teams.

### Thought Leadership

Long-form content like whitepapers establishes authority, improves SEO, and generates leads.

## Metrics and Tools

Garrison recommends tracking KPIs and applying the 80/20 rule—typically 20% of website pages drive most traffic. He recommends four tools:

- **Clearscope:** Uses NLP to analyze top-ranking content and suggest SEO optimizations
- **ChatGPT:** Explains keywords and generates content frameworks for refinement
- **Grammarly:** Corrects passive voice and grammar issues in AI-generated text
- **DALL-E:** Generates images for long-form content, which streamlines short-form video creation

---

**Key Takeaway:** Successful content marketing requires strategy, audience understanding, and leveraging trends like influencer partnerships, voice search optimization, and authentic community building.
