# Digital Marketing - The Power of Now

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/April-2023/Digital-Marketing-The-Power-of-Now  
**Date:** April 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

WTE Solutions employs a multi-channel marketing strategy encompassing social media, content marketing, SEO, PPC advertising, and email campaigns. The company uses tools like Buffer and AI technologies such as ChatGPT to manage content creation and analysis. A recent KPI meeting revealed insights about the importance of responsive, real-time digital marketing.

## The Power of Now by Eckart Tolle

The post references Eckart Tolle's spiritual philosophy book, explaining how "the present moment is all that truly exists." Key concepts include:

1. **Now** – Full consciousness of the present creates happiness
2. **Ego** – Human suffering relates to ego and "pain-bodies"
3. **Mindfulness** – Observing thoughts without judgment enables freedom
4. **Enlightenment** – Recognizing time's illusion through present-moment awareness

The author draws parallels between Tolle's teachings and digital marketing strategy, suggesting that "online enlightenment requires observing what is happening without judgment."

## Now Digital Marketing

Analytics revealed that timely content—specifically Tech News Tuesday and Flipboard Friday roundups—outperformed posts about trending topics like ChatGPT. This demonstrates the "proximity effect," where brands gain legitimacy through association with current events and news.

The company commits to creating fresh, scheduled content while staying responsive to emerging developments. An upcoming web design whitepaper will discuss RIA (responsive, interactive, accessible) design, unifying three previously separate web design categories into one integrated approach.
