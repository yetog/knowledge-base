# Embrace Humanity Thanks to the AT&T Outage

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/February-2024/Embrace-Humanity-Thanks-to-the-AT-T-Outage  
**Date:** February 2024  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

The article opens by framing the AT&T outage as "a stark reminder of our vulnerability" and discusses how systems we depend on can fail unexpectedly. Garrison uses this incident as a metaphor for life's unpredictability.

The core message emphasizes that modern life relies on countless interconnected systems and people. When disruptions occur, we're reminded that "connections, whether powered by technology or the human touch, are precious and, at times, precariously balanced."

Garrison argues the outage offers lessons in humility and resilience. He encourages readers to recognize our shared interdependence and to appreciate those working behind the scenes to restore services.

**Key Point:** The piece concludes by pivoting to cybersecurity, noting that even major corporations face threats. Garrison uses this to promote WTE Solutions' IT Services, positioning cybersecurity as essential for business continuity.
