# Flipboard Beats Twitter

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/November-2022/Flipboard-Beats-Twitter  
**Date:** November 2022  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

WTE Solutions departed Twitter months earlier to concentrate resources on platforms better suited to their team's expertise: Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. The decision stemmed from recognizing a mismatch rather than anticipating platform changes—the organization simply lacked the capacity for consistent Twitter engagement.

The company launched a new website featuring six curated Flipboard magazines covering technology, entrepreneurship, design, and marketing topics. Each magazine focuses on specific content verticals including cloud computing, artificial intelligence, startups, and digital marketing strategy.

## Why Flipboard?

The organization selected Flipboard as their primary content aggregator because it operates as a "content first" platform rather than emphasizing comments like Twitter. The magazine-based format prioritizes content discovery through curator-selected stories organized by topic.

Key advantages include the card-based design system, straightforward curation features, and publisher capabilities via RSS feed integration. The platform enables teams to collaborate on magazines while maintaining organized content presentation.

The author requests feedback on social media aggregation tools, asking readers about their selection criteria and desired improvements for platforms like Buffer or Hootsuite.

Flipboard's social team shared the post on Twitter, connecting the author with team members who demonstrated genuine engagement and professional courtesy.
