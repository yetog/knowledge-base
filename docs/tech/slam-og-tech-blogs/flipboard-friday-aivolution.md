# Flipboard Friday - AIvolution hits the WTE Blog

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/March-2023/Flipboard-Friday-AIvolution  
**Date:** March 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Introduction

The piece opens with a nostalgic reference to *Lost in Space*, drawing parallels between the Robot's warnings ("Danger, Danger, Will Robinson") and current concerns about artificial intelligence advancement. The author questions whether calls for pausing AI development—particularly from figures like Elon Musk—are genuinely motivated or hypocritical.

## The AIvolution Genie

Smith argues that expecting to halt an ongoing revolution is unrealistic, likening it to putting a genie back in a bottle. He notes that Musk's wealth derives partly from technological advantages Tesla possesses, making his anti-AI advocacy seem contradictory.

The post defines "AIvolution" as "the AI revolution that's been going on for years quietly in rooms developers knew about" before generative language models caught public attention. The author has used ChatGPT-enabled tools for years but acknowledges widespread enthusiasm only emerged when AI could simulate human conversation.

## Current Regulatory Pushback

Recent developments included:
- Elon Musk and others requesting a pause in AI development
- Congressional discussions about regulation
- Italy effectively banning ChatGPT over data privacy concerns

Smith expresses frustration with regulatory interference, arguing individuals should decide whether to trade personal data for valuable tools rather than governments dictating terms.

## Flipboard Magazine Sections

**Sign of the Times:** Coverage of AI backlash and regulatory responses

**Start Me Up:** Discussion of startup strategy, featuring Prof. G Scott Galloway's analysis of companies "zigging when others zag"—suggesting innovation shouldn't solely depend on ChatGPT-related features

**Techno:** Microsoft's increased AI investments, ChatGPT integration across products, and potential revenue-sharing with "select publishers" regarding Bing advertising

**Call To Action:** Google News optimization strategies, noting that "Google News sends more than 24 billion clicks down range"

**WTE Ecosystem:** Analysis of why online businesses fail, citing competition, inadequate audience research, poor customer support, and website quality issues. Smith suggests adding SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) to this assessment.

## Pop Shop

The post concludes with appreciation for Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama, emphasizing her distinctive visual language featuring polka dots and organic forms, and her willingness to address mental health and identity through immersive, interactive installations.
