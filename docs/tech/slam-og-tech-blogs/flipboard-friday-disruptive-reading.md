# Flipboard Friday: Disruptive Reading

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/May-2023/Flipboard-Friday-Disruptive-Reading  
**Date:** May 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

The post explores how established companies can be challenged through disruptive innovation, drawing lessons from two key books: Chris Heivly's *Build The Fort* and Clayton Christensen's *The Innovator's Dilemma*.

## Build The Fort

The author uses childhood fort-building as an analogy for startup strategy. Key takeaways include:

- Using familiar concepts to reduce market shock when introducing innovations
- Understanding that venture capitalists invest in teams rather than products alone
- Building capable teams while identifying needed resources
- Creating Minimal Viable Products (MVPs) to gather customer feedback quickly
- Using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure real-world results
- Learning "fast, adjust, and then make a bet" when time-constrained

## The Innovator's Dilemma

Five strategies for startups to compete against larger corporations:

1. **Focus on Disruptive Innovations** — Target niches or underserved segments; create alternatives offering lower costs, simplicity, or novel features

2. **Target Non-consumers** — Attack where established companies overlook opportunities, such as sustainability gaps

3. **Be Nimble** — Leverage speed and rapid iteration to counter giants' scale advantages

4. **Cannibalize Yourself** — Innovate continuously without protecting existing products

5. **Adopt Long-Term Perspective** — Accept that disruption requires patience before profitability

The author emphasizes using metrics to validate assumptions and avoiding unproductive approaches.
