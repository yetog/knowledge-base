# Flipboard Friday: Eureka A New ChatGPT Content Marketing Trick

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/May-2023/Flipboard-Friday-Eureka-A-New-ChatGPT-Content-Marketing-Trick  
**Date:** May 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Introduction

The post explores how ChatGPT is transforming content creation and digital marketing practices. The author describes using OpenAI's ChatGPT alongside complementary tools like Clearscope and Grammarly since December of the previous year.

## Strange Alchemy: It's All About The Prompts Now

The author emphasizes that successful AI use requires strong prompt engineering skills. As Grace Paley noted, "You write from what you know, but you write into what you don't know." The author illustrates this principle through creating a McKinsey Consulting-style report, where understanding the consulting firm's voice enabled better ChatGPT prompts. Effective prompt engineering demands testing, refinement, and learning from failures.

## ChatGPT's Layered Learning

The progression from simple Q&A to sophisticated content demonstrates improved efficiency. Early work required approximately 80% human editing; GPT-4's capabilities reduced this significantly. The author highlights brainstorming as ChatGPT's most valuable feature.

**Example prompt exchange:** The author asked ChatGPT for a gonzo journalist's sidekick name, receiving "Razor Ramble" as a response. Following queries explored what vehicle and character traits suited this fictional companion. These conversations informed creative content development without directly copying responses.

## Genius Lunch

Collaborating with Phil Buckley, the author discovered process optimization: feeding Clearscope's keyword output directly into ChatGPT, then polishing with Grammarly. This workflow eliminated redundant steps, reducing content creation time from a full day to a single morning—saving 4-8 hours daily.

## What is Clearscope

Clearscope uses AI and machine learning for SEO optimization, offering:

- **Keyword Research:** Identifies high-traffic keywords for specific topics
- **Content Grading:** Provides real-time feedback (A+++ rating system)
- **Recommendations:** Suggests keyword optimization and structural improvements
- **Competitor Analysis:** Compares content against competitors
- **Reporting:** Tracks performance metrics over time

The author describes this tool as exceptionally valuable despite significant costs, emphasizing that time investment matters considerably given personal health challenges.

## Phil's Midjourney Discussion

Phil created images using Midjourney text-to-image generation. The post includes a detailed Midjourney prompt and references LinkedIn discussions about the tool's applications for various creative workflows.

---

**Key Takeaway:** Effective AI content creation combines prompt engineering skill, process optimization, and complementary tools rather than relying on automation alone.
