## Riffing With Einstein & Socrates

The author enjoyed curating Fionna Agomuch's piece about Character.AI into WTE Solutions' Sign-of-the-Times magazine. The post highlights the platform's ability to deliver responses that stay true to historical figures' perspectives.

When asked "What is network as a service?" through Character.AI:

**Albert Einstein's response** explained that network as a service represents cloud infrastructure allowing companies to access modern technology and communications resources without maintaining their own infrastructure.

**Socrates's response** deconstructed the concept philosophically, defining a network as devices transmitting and receiving information, and service as helpful activity. He concluded that network as a service combines these elements with the intention to help others.
