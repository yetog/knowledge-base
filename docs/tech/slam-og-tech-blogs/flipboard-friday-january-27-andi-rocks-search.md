# Flipboard Friday January 27 - Andi Rocks Search

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/January-2023/Flipboard-Friday-January-27-Andi-Rocks-Search  
**Date:** January 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Introduction

The author shares enthusiasm about discovering an article by JR Raphael in Fast Company titled "This intelligent new search site is like a mashup of Google and ChatGPT," describing it as a significant moment worthy of immediate attention.

## Search - It's The Experience Stupid

The author acknowledges being an early advocate for ChatGPT's impact on search. Key observations include:

- ChatGPT reduced personal Google searches by more than half
- The appeal lies in the engaging, conversational experience rather than traditional search mechanics
- ChatGPT's presentation "was FUN, engaging, and flowed"
- The tool prompted natural follow-up questions

## About Andi

Andi represents a Y Combinator-backed AI search startup founded by Angela Hoover (CEO, age 24) and Jed White (veteran tech professional). The platform employs a hybrid structure:

- Left side: ChatGPT-like answers with attribution
- Right side: Traditional SEO results

## The Author's Workflow

Since December, the author's research methodology:
- Begins with ChatGPT for technical questions
- Supplements with Google search when needed
- Values speed and engagement over traditional search results

## Closing Question

The author invites reader feedback on whether Andi's hybrid approach represents the future of search, requesting comments or emails to msmith@wte.net.
