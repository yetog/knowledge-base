# Flipboard Friday: Martin's Tips To Boost Your Creativity

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/June-2023/Flipboard-Friday-Martin-s-Tips-To-Boost-Your-Creativity  
**Date:** June 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## The AI Creativity Free Lunch Myth

The author challenges the notion that artificial intelligence provides effortless creativity. He argues that "AI is not a free lunch because it is all about the prompts," emphasizing that quality AI outputs depend on the expertise and experience of the person using it. ChatGPT functions as a tool—like a painter's brush—requiring skilled hands to produce meaningful results. The premise is that AI remains neutral and cannot independently generate breakthrough ideas.

## Martin's Marketing Creativity Tips

The author presents five principles for cultivating creativity:

### 1. Work Beats Inspiration

Action produces more creativity than contemplation. Actual creation through writing, painting, or brainstorming generates results superior to passive thinking alone. The author contends that creativity isn't an innate trait but a skill everyone possesses.

### 2. Inspiration and Thinking Matter

Sustained reflection contributes meaningfully to creative output, though it represents less than ten percent of the final work. The author cites his thirty-year consideration of a cross-country bicycle journey, which became reality after a health crisis prompted action.

### 3. Vulnerability & Courage

Meaningful creativity requires embracing risk and potential failure. The author references Brene Brown's "Daring Greatly" and Colin Tipping's "Radical Forgiveness," advocating self-compassion and authentic vulnerability over polished facades.

### 4. Ego

While confidence is necessary, excessive ego obstructs creativity. Maintaining a "quiet mind" that observes thoughts without judgment facilitates creative breakthroughs.

### 5. Gladwell's 10K Rule

Mastery requires approximately 10,000 hours of deliberate practice. Creativity demands continuous experimentation, failure, and refinement rather than natural talent alone.

## Closing

The author invites readers to share their own creativity insights and provides contact information.
