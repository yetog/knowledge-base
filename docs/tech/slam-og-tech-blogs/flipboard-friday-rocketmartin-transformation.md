# Flipboard Friday: RocketMartin Transformation

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/July-2023/Flipboard-Friday-RocketMartin-Transformation  
**Date:** July 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Opening

The author reflects on Michael Panerella's Forbes article about digital transformation, highlighting the insight that "innovation strategy is centered on generating new ideas via unconventional thinking," rather than technology itself.

## People Transform Not Technology

Martin shares his personal journey with technology, beginning with the Apple II in 1984. Growing up dyslexic in 1960s Texas, he struggled with conventional learning methods. However, technology—specifically spreadsheets and graphics—transformed how he processed information, converting abstract numbers into visual stories. He credits his mother's advocacy and "work harder" philosophy with helping him overcome early obstacles.

The author emphasizes that creativity emerges from struggle: "creativity thrives in the gaps, in the spaces where conventional methods fail." His unconventional thinking style, born from learning challenges, ultimately became an asset.

## The Gladwell Rules

Drawing from Malcolm Gladwell's work, Martin identifies three principles:

### The Myth of the Free Lunch

Echoing the 10,000-hour rule, success requires persistent effort with no shortcuts. Martin's daily writing output reflects this dedication, accumulating nearly 900,000 Grammarly words. He notes that even with AI assistance, genuine creativity demands sustained labor.

### The Power of Curiosity

Everything contains a story. When communication fails, the narrative requires deeper exploration. Vulnerability and curiosity unlock meaning.

### The Art of Storytelling

"Facts inform, but stories transform." Despite AI tools' value, human-centered narratives remain essential.

## The New WTE About Page

Martin describes using AI to craft WTE's updated About page, comparing the process to his father's approach to golf—a daily discipline. He emphasizes that no masterpiece emerges directly from chatbots; human touch remains irreplaceable.

Recent conversations with IBM SEO expert Phil Buckley and Moon-Audo founder Nichole Baird shaped Martin's vision of modular content "seamlessly connected by natural language processing." ChatGPT represents a transformative tool, making previously impossible tasks achievable.

## Closing Question

Martin asks readers to consider: How do we transform and improve? Does technology change us, or do we change technology?
