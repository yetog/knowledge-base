# Flipboard Friday: The SMB Marketing Automation Challenge

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/July-2023/Flipboard-Friday-The-SMB-Marketing-Automation-Challenge  
**Date:** July 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Introduction

The author discusses how a previous post about marketing automation gained significant traction, prompting him to explore the SMB Marketing Automation Challenge further. He reflects on purchasing his first Apple II computer in 1984 to manage paperwork and connects this experience to modern marketing's ongoing struggle with repetitive tasks.

## What is Marketing?

Marketing automation encompasses software tools that streamline repetitive marketing tasks and boost team efficiency. The author highlights marketing's inherent paradox: professionals spend only 10% of their time on creative work while devoting 90% to tedious administrative activities.

The post defines marketing as "the art and science of understanding and satisfying customers' needs." Key marketing functions include:

- **Market Research:** Understanding target audiences, preferences, and behaviors
- **Product Development:** Creating offerings informed by customer insights
- **Campaigns:** Email drip campaigns, social ads, and social media initiatives
- **Sales and Distribution:** Leveraging digital-first interactions
- **Customer Relationship Management (CRM):** Managing customer data and engagement
- **Analysis and Adjustments:** Identifying patterns amid marketing noise

## The CRM Marketing Automation Challenge

The author notes frustration with expensive platforms like Pardot and Marketo, which pose steep learning curves and high costs unsuitable for small and medium-sized businesses. He emphasizes that "marketing automation tools segment customers into different demographic, psychographic, and behavioral groups."

Current marketing automation represents a $4.5 billion market, yet remains inaccessible to many SMBs due to complexity and expense.

## Social Media's Impact

Social media marketing can dramatically amplify demand unpredictably. The author warns that viral posts create unexpected operational challenges requiring rapid response capabilities. Tools like Buffer help teams schedule posts and monitor engagement efficiently.

## The SMB Digital First Gold Rush

The author traces the typical journey SMBs undergo:

1. Building their first website
2. Expecting immediate ROI
3. Learning web design's impact on conversion
4. Developing creative campaigns
5. Becoming overwhelmed by demand
6. Needing marketing automation solutions

## Marketing Automation Tools Currently Used

The author employs several tools:

- **ChatGPT:** Generates initial content drafts, though robot-like output requires substantial revision
- **ClearScope:** AI-powered SEO tool that identifies keywords and optimizes content (costs approximately $750 monthly)
- **DALL-E & Photoshop AI:** Generates images for content

## Feedback and Recommendations

John Kean, cited as a friend and expert, recommends using Zapier for automation workflows alongside tools like Apollo.io, Smartlead.ai, and HubSpot for freelance marketing operations.

## Conclusion

The author advocates for user-friendly, affordable marketing automation solutions designed specifically for creative professionals and SMBs, rather than enterprise-focused platforms.
