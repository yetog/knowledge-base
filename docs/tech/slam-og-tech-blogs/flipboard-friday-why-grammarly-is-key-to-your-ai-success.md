# Flipboard Friday: Why Grammarly Is Key To Your AI Success

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/April-2023/Flipboard-Friday-Why-Grammarly-Is-Key-To-Your-AI-Success  
**Date:** April 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

## Introduction

Grammarly leverages artificial intelligence to correct spelling and grammar effectively. However, the AI writing assistant proves crucial for using ChatGPT and similar tools because generative pre-trained transformers make consistent mistakes in several areas.

## Common ChatGPT Mistakes

The article identifies four primary issues:

**Passive Voice** - ChatGPT frequently generates passive constructions requiring conversion to active voice for better online communication.

**Stop Words** - Natural language generators overuse SEO "stop" words like "it" and "they," particularly in sequential bullet points, creating ambiguity for search engines.

**ChatGPT Tone** - The AI produces recognizable, formulaic content with phrases like "can be seen" that sound stiff and robotic, lacking authentic brand voice.

**Rule Exception** - Direct ChatGPT content should be labeled with OpenAI logos and blockquote tags for transparency.

## Why Passive Voice Doesn't Work Online

The piece explains that web communication exists in "a constant state of NOWnews," making passive constructions feel outdated. Active voice focuses on doers rather than recipients, aligning with searchers' hunter-gatherer psychology and reducing cognitive dissonance.

## How Grammarly Helps ChatGPT

The author identifies six key reasons Grammarly supports AI tool usage:

1. **Natural Language Understanding (NLU)** - Machine learning enables deep comprehension of grammar, syntax, and semantics.

2. **Continuous Improvement** - User interactions and crowdsourced feedback accelerate AI learning capabilities.

3. **Human-AI Collaboration** - Tools work together rather than replacing human effort; GPT-4 regularly achieves Grammarly scores in the nineties.

4. **Integration** - Seamless incorporation into everyday applications makes AI usage increasingly invisible.

5. **Personalization** - Grammarly Insights provides customized writing analytics compared to broader user cohorts.

6. **AIaaS** - Cloud-based scaling enables millions of simultaneous users through advanced AI models.

## GrammarlyGo Section

Grammarly introduced GrammarlyGo, a new AI writing assistant competing with ChatGPT. When tested on web development impact reporting, the author rated its output as "about a C grade," noting it performs better for emails and thank-you notes than comprehensive content generation.

## Conclusion

The author emphasizes that Grammarly remains essential to AI success because it enables faster, better, and more abundant writing—critical given the web's endless demands for fresh content.
