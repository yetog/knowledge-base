# Flipboard Friday - Why Video Content is King

**Author:** Martin W Smith

## What Google Wants

In the author's early e-commerce experience, he learned that understanding Google's priorities was essential. Google favored educational content alongside sales, which created challenges for commerce-focused sites. The author notes that organic search represented half of his company's six million dollars in annual revenue, making Google's preferences critical to success.

Google Analytics revealed that the company's limited video content performed exceptionally well as "sticky content," keeping visitors engaged longer than other material types.

## Why Video Content is King in the Digital Age

Building on Bill Gates' 1996 observation that "content is king," the article argues video has become the supreme content format. YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat, and Instagram Reels have become primary content consumption hubs.

Videos excel at conveying complex information quickly and memorably because humans are visual learners. Whether tutorials, explainers, or short-form content, videos make information more digestible and shareable than text alone.

## Video Marketing SEO Advantages

Search engines prioritize high-quality video content. With proper optimization — keyword headings, descriptions, and links — videos significantly boost organic traffic. The author's experience demonstrates that videos dramatically outperform other content in engagement metrics, suggesting the company will expand video production.

## Harnessing the Power of Video: A Guide for Your Websites

Three primary strategies for integrating video on websites include:

**Create and use a Video Hosting Tool:** Platforms like Brightcove allow complete control over narrative, pacing, visuals, and eliminate distracting advertisements or links to competitors' content.

**Create and use YouTube and other Social Platforms:** YouTube and Vimeo offer massive audiences but intense competition. Wide distribution comes with visibility challenges.

**Embed OPV (Other People's Videos) with Your Copy:** Embedding relevant third-party videos enriches content. While credit flows to the original creator through embedded frames, websites gain engagement time benefits — a mutually advantageous arrangement.

**Hybrid Videos - Their Content Supports Yours:** Videos combining original commentary with royalty-free clips from sources like Pexels create economical, legally compliant content. The author credits Kelly's animations and stock footage while supporting creators through attribution and donations.

## Navigating Copyright

Attribution reflects integrity and respect for creators. The author utilizes Pexels Creative Commons and other royalty-free resources. Proper attribution — including linking to creator profiles — acknowledges the collaborative nature of modern content creation.

## Telling Effective Video Stories - YouTubers

A Native American proverb frames the power of storytelling: "Tell me facts and I learn. Tell me truth and I believe. But tell me a story and it will live in my heart forever."

**LegalEagle's Video Storytelling:** LegalEagle, hosted by attorney Devin, exemplifies exceptional video storytelling by making legal content engaging. His team member Amy, now running Awkward Anthems, demonstrates how production expertise translates across platforms, rapidly building audiences.

Key storytelling elements include:

- **Branding:** Distinct visual identities — Devin's sharp suits and formal office backdrop versus Amy's casual signature style — create recognizable brand experiences
- **Calls-to-Action:** Directing viewers toward specific actions prevents audience confusion. Effective CTAs guide engagement
- **Pattern Interrupts:** Dynamic elements like jokes, graphics, and visual changes maintain viewer attention throughout longer content
- **Sponsor Segues:** Integrating advertisements naturally without disrupting content flow requires skill
- **Production Mindset:** Maintaining energy, managing time, and adapting to production challenges demand rhythm and resilience

## Video Marketing Challenges & Examples

### Technical Challenges

**Serving & Playing:** High video consumption demands substantial bandwidth. Professional hosting services like Brightcove mitigate site performance impacts.

**Formatting:** Video-to-player dimension mismatches create black bars. Proper format selection prevents this distraction.

**Gear:** Video production equipment investments motivate continued content creation. The author references educational resources like Ehud Sopher's equipment guidance video.

### Success Examples

**Airbnb** leverages user-generated video testimonials showcasing accommodations and travel experiences, enticing potential customers through visual storytelling.

**TED** structures its entire website around expert video presentations organized by topic, facilitating discovery of relevant content.

**HubSpot** integrates video tutorials, courses, and webinars into educational resources, helping users master platform tools while exploring related marketing concepts.

**GoPro** crowdsources user-generated content, featuring creators using their cameras in extreme situations — skydivers, cliff skiers, mountaineers — demonstrating product capabilities through authentic user experiences.

The article concludes by noting Red Bull represents another exemplary case study, with future analysis exploring how organizations function as media companies.
