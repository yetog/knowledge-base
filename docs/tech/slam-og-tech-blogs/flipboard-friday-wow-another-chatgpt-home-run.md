# Flipboard Friday: WOW Another ChatGPT Home Run

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/June-2023/Flipboard-Friday-WOW-Another-ChatGPT-Home-Run  
**Date:** June 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

ChatGPT continues to surprise WTE Solutions' team. While preparing a report in Cincinnati, the author discovered their most popular June post appeared without a known human author. The mystery resolved when developers revealed they had successfully automated content creation using OpenAI's API, directing ChatGPT responses directly onto the website.

The author explains that OpenAI offers two distinct tools: the OpenAI Playground for technical/analytical work ("left-brain") and ChatGPT for marketing purposes ("right-brain"). WTE's coding team leveraged the Playground to script prompts and publish generated content, achieving rapid Google visibility.

## The Cody AI Surprise

The ghost-authored post covered "MeetCody.ai: The Next-level AI-Powered Solution Transforming Tech," which became June's top performer. CEO/CTO Eric had been "playing around" with this technology. The author notes that Cody could revolutionize internal website search, traditionally plagued by tuning difficulties and poor user experience compared to conversational AI interfaces.

## Cody's Left & Right Brain

While developers refine Cody's API, the marketing team recognizes an additional advantage: automated content testing reveals which topics resonate most with audiences. This data-driven approach helps digital teams prioritize limited resources effectively.

The post references multiple prior ChatGPT-related articles, noting the extensive time marketing teams have invested explaining the AI revolution as it unfolds.
