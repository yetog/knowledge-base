## Introduction

The author describes initiating "Genius Lunch" conversations inspired by colleague Phil Buckley's concept. He explains his motivation: "I have cancer" and seeks to share survival strategies while requesting assistance building a network of smart individuals for meaningful discussions.

## Surviving Cancer Tips

The author has Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL), treated at multiple institutions over seventeen years. He offers five survival strategies:

1. **Be Kind** - Maintain patience and treat medical professionals as allies despite frustration and stress
2. **Crowdsource Your Cancer** - Seek treatment at institutions actively researching your specific condition rather than defaulting to nearby hospitals
3. **Donate** - Contribute to treating institutions to build relationships with development teams and hospital leadership
4. **Toxins** - Eliminate harmful influences, including negative self-talk and toxic relationships
5. **Friends and Family** - Acknowledge that emotional support from loved ones proves essential alongside medical care
