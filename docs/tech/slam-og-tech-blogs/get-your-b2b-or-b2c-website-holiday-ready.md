# Get Your B2B or B2C Website Holiday Ready!

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/September-2023/Get-Your-B2B-or-B2C-Website-Holiday-Ready  
**Date:** September 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

The piece opens by emphasizing the holiday season as a commercial opportunity for companies to engage customers and increase revenue. The author notes, "The holiday season is more than just twinkling lights and festive cheer; it's a golden opportunity for your company to shine, engage customers, and boost sales."

The post targets both B2B enterprises seeking year-end success and B2C retailers preparing for peak shopping periods like Black Friday. The author states: "I love the holidays because my software development and web design company's toys come out and play."

## Content Sections

The article organizes resources into categories:

**Masterclasses** covering digital strategy, web design, and SEO optimization

**Branding & Video Marketing** featuring Simon Sinek's "Start with Why" philosophy, Bugatti case studies, Instagram Reels strategies, and emphasis on video content's dominance

**Security** addressing SSL certificates, HTTPS implementation, and backup solutions

**B2B** resources on service sales and marketing automation

**B2C** guidance on sales tax compliance, Amazon Buy with Prime integration, and ecommerce modernization

**Tools** exploring ChatGPT applications, web accessibility standards, and AI writing assistance

The article concludes by inviting readers to engage with WTE Solutions for holiday website optimization.
