# How Instagram Reels Can Grow Your Business

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/How-Instagram-Reels-Can-Grow-Your-Business-1  
**Date:** February 2022  
**Author:** Patrick

---

## Introduction

Instagram Reels, a relatively new feature similar to TikTok, enables businesses to create and share short-form video content with their followers and beyond their existing social circles.

## What Are Instagram Reels?

Instagram Reels is described as "an Instagram feature that allows users to create video content to share with their followers or anyone else who uses the app." The platform provides creators with various audios and effects for video editing, making content creation accessible.

## Business Growth Benefits

The post explains that the Reels algorithm favors engagement. When published, videos enter a dedicated feed where users discover content matching their interests, significantly expanding potential audience reach. This growth is characterized as organic, requiring less advertising spend than traditional promotional methods.

## Practical Applications

Businesses use Reels to showcase products through demonstrations, creation processes, and behind-the-scenes footage. Examples include food trucks displaying preparation methods and retail businesses offering product insights. Service-based companies can explain their offerings through video.

## Success Strategies

Studies indicate Reels perform best with high-quality video resolution and trending audio. "Using trending audios increases the chances of your Reel being shown to more people and showing up on an increased number of newsfeeds."

## Conclusion

Reels is an accessible tool for business growth, and online presence is customer-expected in modern commerce.
