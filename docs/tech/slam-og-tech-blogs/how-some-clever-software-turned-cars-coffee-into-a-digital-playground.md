# How Some Clever Software Turned Cars & Coffee into a Digital Playground (and got our CEO into the rare Mustang GTD)

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/How-Some-Clever-Software-Turned-Cars-Coffee-into-a-Digital-Playground  
**Date:** September 2025  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

The article opens with a vivid scene at Cars & Coffee Morrisville—a Saturday morning gathering filled with premium vehicles and enthusiasts. The WTE Solutions CTO attended to showcase how backend technology transforms a beloved car show into a "tech-driven community hub."

## Cars & Coffee Morrisville: More Than a Morning Cruise

Held monthly at the Imperial Center in Morrisville, North Carolina, this event attracts car enthusiasts with Porsches, Lamborghinis, and classic muscle cars. Beyond vehicles, the gathering centers on community ritual: food trucks, vendor merchandise, and story-sharing. However, managing such a growing event presents logistical challenges.

## Behind the Curtain: WTE's Tech Touch

### Rideology.io: A Garage in the Cloud

This "community-in-a-box" platform enables users to create car profiles, track modifications, connect with fellow enthusiasts, and share vehicles digitally. Built using elements from Kentico and Umbraco content management systems, it scales efficiently while maintaining engagement.

### Moblz: Keeping the Wheels (and Coffee) Turning

This platform manages food truck and vendor logistics, scheduling, and operations to streamline the event experience.

## Results

Since launch, Rideology.io achieved:

- Over 65,000 views
- 185,000 interactions
- Active user-generated profiles and sharing
- Directory features accounting for nearly 8% of page views
- High engagement through gamification and brand ambassador programs

## Conclusion

The piece concludes that successful community building requires blending cultural values with thoughtful technology, ultimately asking whether online platforms can capture the soul of physical gatherings.
