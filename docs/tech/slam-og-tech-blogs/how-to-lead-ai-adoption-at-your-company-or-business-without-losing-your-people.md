# How to Lead AI Adoption at Your Company or Business Without Losing Your People

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/October-2025/How-to-Lead-AI-Adoption-at-Your-Company-or-Business-Without-Losing-Your-People  
**Date:** October 2025  
**Author:** Eric Garrison, Founder and CEO, WTE Solutions

---

## Overview

Garrison emphasizes that successful AI adoption depends on leadership approach rather than technology alone. He notes: "AI is powerful and tempting, but it is not a magic switch." The central thesis addresses the gap between AI investment and actual organizational maturity.

## The Leadership Challenge

Companies often treat AI as a feature launch rather than organizational transformation. When leaders prioritize tools over outcomes, frontline adoption suffers. Garrison stresses designing work around people first, technology second.

## The FRAME Framework

A structured approach with five components:

- **Frame** the problem in human terms
- **Reveal** the plan and governance publicly
- **Assess** skills and design role-specific training
- **Measure** using meaningful metrics and iterate
- **Empower** teams with safe experimentation spaces

## 30-60-90 Day Pilot Plan

- **Days 1-30:** Shadow testing with human approval
- **Days 30-60:** Co-pilot mode with selective automation
- **Days 60-90:** Assessment and scaling decision

## Key Metrics

Track adoption rate, time saved per user, quality improvements, user confidence, and business outcomes.

## Governance Approach

Lightweight oversight emphasizing data clarity, role-based access, and early involvement of legal and security teams.

## Common Pitfalls

Pilots without executive sponsors, theoretical training, premature scaling, and overly restrictive access controls undermine success.
