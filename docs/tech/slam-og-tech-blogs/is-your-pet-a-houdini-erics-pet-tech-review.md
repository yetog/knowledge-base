# Is Your Pet a Houdini? Eric's Pet Tech Review

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/November-2023/Is-Your-Pet-a-Houdini-Eric-s-Pet-Tech-Review  
**Date:** November 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

Eric Garrison lives on ten acres in rural North Carolina and shares his experience managing pets prone to escaping. He explores three pet tracking technologies suitable for owners seeking security solutions.

## Ring's Pet Tracker: QR Code Approach

Ring offers a simple tracking solution using QR codes attached to collars. When someone scans the code, they access the owner's contact information and pet health data through Ring's app. The system costs approximately ten dollars but lacks GPS or wireless capabilities. Effectiveness depends on community members scanning and reporting findings.

## Findster: No-Subscription GPS Tracking

Findster provides autonomous GPS tracking without monthly subscription fees. Owners attach the device to their pet's collar and monitor location and activity through a smartphone application. The tracker offers pinpoint accuracy, robust tracking range, and waterproof design, making it budget-friendly for real-time tracking needs.

## FI Collar: Advanced Multi-Satellite Tracking

The FI Series 3 represents the most sophisticated option, utilizing three satellite systems — GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo — for pinpointing pets within six feet. It features LTE-M cellular network technology reaching 30% farther than conventional networks and costs approximately $3.20 weekly. The device withstands harsh conditions and offers enhanced signal sensitivity for remote locations.

## Conclusion

Pet tech solutions provide safety and peace of mind across various budgets and technological preferences, combining modern satellite technology with smartphone convenience.
