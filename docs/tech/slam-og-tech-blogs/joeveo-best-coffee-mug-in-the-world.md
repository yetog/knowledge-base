# Joeveo - Best Coffee Mug in the World

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/March-2023/Joeveo-Best-Coffee-Mug-in-the-World  
**Date:** March 2023  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

The article explores what makes an exceptional coffee mug, emphasizing that these vessels deserve as much consideration as coffee makers themselves. The author discusses the ideal characteristics: double-wall construction with vacuum insulation and heat retention, proper fit in cup holders, and ergonomic design.

The piece centers on Dean Verhoeven, the engineer behind Joeveo, a stainless steel travel mug featuring phase-change material technology. The author describes meeting Verhoeven and being impressed by his meticulous approach to manufacturing—from drilling precise holes for thermal regulation to crafting wooden box toggles.

## Meeting Brilliant People

Compares Verhoeven's engineering passion to the author's engineer father, noting his ability to identify consumer needs that people struggle to articulate.

## Joeveo Coffee Mug Drinking Experience

Details the mug's practical features—the rubber logo band preventing drops, substantial yet lightweight construction, and superior insulation. The author expresses that Verhoeven's craftsmanship rivals that of innovators like Howard Hughes or Steve Jobs.

---

The article concludes by acknowledging trade-offs: hand-washing recommended and purchasing directly from Joeveo.com rather than mass retailers. The author invites readers to share alternative favorite mugs via email.
