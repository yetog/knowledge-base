# North Carolina: Where Collabs Spark Innovation

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/December-2023/North-Carolina-Where-Collabs-Spark-Innovation  
**Date:** December 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

North Carolina has established itself as a research and technology hub. The state's defining characteristic, however, is its collaborative ethos — where different sectors unite to develop innovative solutions that reshape industries.

## Epic Games + LEGO

A notable partnership united LEGO (based in Billund, Denmark) with Epic Games (headquartered in Cary, North Carolina). Their collaboration merged physical and digital entertainment through Fortnite and LEGO products. Users can construct physical LEGO structures that transfer into the Fortnite virtual environment, combining tactile creativity with modern gameplay mechanics.

The article references LEGO's organizational purpose: "The LEGO Group's mission is to inspire and develop the builders of tomorrow through the power of play."

The piece suggests this represents a growing trend where physical and digital experiences increasingly intersect.

## IBM & Red Hat

IBM and Red Hat developed "Mesh Cloud," integrating public, private, and edge computing environments. This distributed architecture addresses enterprise challenges including security concerns, system scalability, and technological complexity while permitting multi-environment application management.

## Conclusion

The article argues these collaborations demonstrate how diverse organizations generate outcomes exceeding individual contributions. North Carolina's collaborative culture positions it as a global innovation leader where competitive rivals become partners and obstacles transform into opportunities.
