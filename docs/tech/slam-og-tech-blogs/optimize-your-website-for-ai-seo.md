# Optimize Your Website for AI SEO

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/May-2023/Optimize-Your-Website-for-AI-SEO  
**Date:** May 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

The article addresses the necessity of optimizing websites for AI alongside traditional SEO. It emphasizes that "always keep your customer experience, marketing strategy, and target audience at the forefront" when developing content strategies. The piece advocates balancing excellent user experience with SEO optimization, noting that quality customer interactions ultimately drive both backlinks and engagement.

## Optimize for AI SEO

This section provides eleven actionable optimization strategies:

**Structured Data:** Implement Schema.org markup to help AI algorithms categorize and understand website content more effectively.

**Semantic HTML:** Use proper heading hierarchies and semantic tags to provide organizational clarity for AI systems.

**Content Quality:** Write clearly and concisely, avoiding technical jargon that confuses both algorithms and users.

**Natural Language:** The author warns against directly publishing AI-generated content, stating that "Google penalizes duplicate content" and detecting AI-written material risks algorithmic penalties when multiple sites publish identical outputs.

**Accessible Media:** Provide alt text for images and video transcripts, noting this benefits "eighty million Americans with disabilities."

**Mobile Optimization:** Ensure responsive design works across all devices and platforms.

**Internal Linking:** Create logical link structures showing topical relationships.

**XML Sitemaps:** Maintain updated sitemaps for crawler navigation.

**Performance:** Optimize loading speeds through image compression, file minification, and CDN usage.

**Social Integration:** Add sharing buttons and social metadata.

**Continuous Improvement:** Regularly audit and update content based on analytics and user feedback.

## RankBrain & User Intent

This section explains that user intent represents "the goals or purpose behind a search query." Four intent types are identified:

- **Informational Intent:** Seeking answers or information (examples: "what is user intent")
- **Navigational Intent:** Visiting specific websites (examples: "Facebook login")
- **Transactional Intent:** Completing purchases or actions (examples: "Buy iPhone 14")
- **Commercial Investigation:** Researching products before buying (examples: "iPhone 13 vs. Samsung Galaxy S22")

The article concludes that aligning content with user intent improves search visibility, drives targeted traffic, and increases engagement.
