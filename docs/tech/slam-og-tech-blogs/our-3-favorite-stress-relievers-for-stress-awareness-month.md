# Our 3 Favorite Stress Relievers for Stress Awareness Month

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Our-3-Favorite-Stress-Relievers-for-Stress-Awareness-Month  
**Date:** March 2022  
**Author:** Rachelle in eMarketing

---

April marks Stress Awareness Month, and WTE Solutions staff members shared their preferred stress-reduction strategies.

## Mindfulness and Relaxation Apps

The post defines mindfulness as "awareness that arises from paying attention with purpose in the present moment and without judgment." Recommended applications include:

- Mindful.org
- Headspace
- Shine
- Yoga Nidra (available on Android and Apple)

## Education

Staying informed about current events reduces anxiety by decreasing panic responses. WTE staff utilize Pryor Learning, a subscription service offering unlimited online courses across thousands of topics.

## Games

Gaming provides mental relief through distraction and enjoyment. Staff recommendations:

- **Tropicats:** A match-3 puzzle game featuring vegetarian cats
- **Happy Color:** A color-by-numbers application mimicking childhood coloring activities
