## Why I Love Apple AirTags

The author shares enthusiasm for Apple AirTags after witnessing a friend nearly discard one due to a depleted battery. The post emphasizes that users shouldn't toss their AirTags — they can simply replace the battery instead.

Apple AirTags are small, coin-shaped tracking devices introduced in April 2021. Users employ them to monitor bags, bikes, and other belongings. The author notes that friends also use them to track children and their possessions.

## Key Features of Apple AirTags

- **Precision Finding:** Works with iPhones and iPads (especially U1-chip models from iPhone 11 onward) to provide exact distance and direction
- **Visual & Audible Feedback:** Real-time guidance to locate misplaced items
- **Lost Mode:** Enables notifications when other Apple devices discover a lost item
- **Contact Information:** NFC-capable devices can access contact details when an AirTag is in Lost Mode
- **Privacy and Security:** Location data remains private and anonymous, even from Apple
- **Anti-stalking Measures:** Alerts users if unknown AirTags travel with them
- **Design and Accessories:** Features free engraving with text and 31 emoji options; compatible with various attachment accessories
- **Battery Life and Water Resistance:** User-replaceable CR2032 watch battery lasting one year; water and dust resistant

AirTags integrate with Apple's Find My network — an encrypted, anonymous system for locating Apple devices. Users manage AirTags through the Find My app on iOS devices.
