# Recession? Harvard Says Great Time for Tech Upgrades

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Recession-Harvard-Says-Great-Time-for-Tech-Upgrades  
**Date:** August 2022  
**Author:** Martin W Smith

---

The piece opens by acknowledging economic headwinds—stock declines, rising interest rates, and inflation—that suggest potential recession ahead. The author notes that while it may seem self-serving for a software development firm to advocate for digital transformation during downturns, Harvard Business Review's research supports this perspective.

## Core Argument

According to the article, "downturns tend to be short-lived and followed by long periods of expansion." Companies that emerge as winners during challenging times are those acquiring assets, customers, talent, and capabilities at favorable prices. Industrial companies especially benefit from accelerated digital transformation when resources are most available.

## Five ROI-Focused Tech Transformations

### 1. Mobile First Website Upgrade

Most companies have responsive websites, but few have progressed to mobile-first design. Modern mobile-first sites leverage cloud storage, APIs, and microservices—loosely coupled applications enabling faster development, independent deployment, and easier maintenance. The company works with AWS, Google, and Microsoft cloud platforms.

### 2. Mobile First Content Marketing

With over 80% of web traffic originating from mobile devices, content strategy must adapt. The article recommends:

- Converting long-form articles into connected short pieces using hybrid accordion menus
- Emphasizing interactivity and user selection
- Prioritizing short-form videos (under one minute)
- Redesigning infographics for smaller screens
- Leveraging platforms like Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube

### 3. Create an Online Community

The 1-9-90 Rule suggests that 1% of visitors contribute content, 9% share it, and 90% consume it. Rather than creating all content themselves, companies should curate user-generated content through community platforms. Rideology.io exemplifies this "Community in a Box" approach with contributor profiles, ambassador programs, gamification, leaderboards, and social rewards.

### 4. Improved About Us Page Stories

"Tell me a story and it will live in my heart forever," the article quotes. Effective About Us pages combine Simon Sinek's "Start with Why" philosophy with authentic storytelling. Companies should explain their purpose and values through narratives that build trust and emotional connection within seconds.

### 5. Security Upgrade

Cybersecurity remains critical during recessions. Many improvements require minimal investment. The article recommends free security audits of websites, APIs, microservices, and applications to identify vulnerabilities before exploitation.

## Closing

The piece emphasizes that solving complex technical challenges requires partnership and specialized expertise to make solutions understandable and actionable.
