# The AI Paradox: Why Development is Easier and Cheaper Than Maintenance

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/July-2023/The-AI-Paradox-Why-Development-is-Easier-and-Cheaper-Than-Maintenance  
**Date:** July 2023  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

The article explores how artificial intelligence and automation are transforming software development and website design across diverse sectors including healthcare, banking, ecommerce, and local government. The author's team has leveraged tools like GitHub Copilot and OpenAI's ChatGPT to streamline coding tasks such as bug fixes and predictive code suggestions.

## AI Making Business More Human

Despite AI's reputation as mechanistic, the author argues that intelligent automation can create more personalized experiences. Machine learning enables systems to understand user preferences and behaviors over time, allowing automated services to become increasingly tailored and responsive to individual needs.

## The Core Paradox

WTE Solutions has evolved from using AI as a coding assistant to developing complete tools, including their AI-enabled AgileSiteLite website builder powered by ChatGPT-4. However, they've identified a critical challenge:

> "developing and deploying ML systems is relatively fast and cheap, but maintaining them over time is difficult and expensive" (Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems).

## Key Insight

Creating and implementing AI solutions often proves faster and more affordable than sustaining these systems long-term. Success requires data-driven decision-making and clear problem definition to manage tradeoffs and avoid accumulating hidden technical costs.

## Conclusion

Organizations must thoughtfully plan AI architecture to maximize benefits while minimizing maintenance burdens.
