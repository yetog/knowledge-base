# The "Take My Money" Tech List: What to Spend Your Christmas Money On

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/January-2026/The-Take-My-Money-Tech-List-What-to-Spend-Your-Christmas-Money-On  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

The piece opens with a relatable scenario about having leftover holiday funds and the temptation to purchase tech gadgets. Garrison, as CEO of WTE Solutions, positions himself as someone with extensive hands-on experience testing technology products.

## Product Recommendations

### 1. Casio AE-1200WHD Watch ($25)

Described as a "legend" among pilots and tech enthusiasts, this affordable timepiece offers world time across five zones, stopwatch functionality, alarm capability, water resistance, and a seven-year battery life. The author emphasizes its reliability and practicality without requiring modern conveniences like Bluetooth or daily charging.

### 2. Fitbit Versa 4

This fitness tracker features GPS, heart rate monitoring, over 40 exercise modes, sleep tracking, and six-day battery longevity. Garrison explains that the device provides actionable feedback for behavior modification through data-driven insights about physical activity and sleep patterns.

### 3. Meta Quest 3S

Positioned as a practical VR headset requiring no wires or external sensors, this device offers double the graphical power of previous models with 128GB storage. The author notes applications extending beyond gaming to virtual meetings, 3D design work, and training simulations.

### 4. Nintendo Switch

Despite being eight years old, this portable gaming console remains popular because of its versatility and Nintendo's exclusive game titles like Zelda and Mario.

## Conclusion

Garrison concludes that these products create incremental improvements in daily life rather than revolutionary changes, emphasizing the value of enjoyment without requiring extensive commitment or infrastructure.
