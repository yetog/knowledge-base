# Top 5 Craziest Gadgets from CES 2026

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Blog/January-2026/Top-5-Craziest-Gadgets-from-CES-2026  
**Date:** January 2026  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

The article presents a curated list of innovative devices showcased at CES 2026. Garrison introduces each gadget with enthusiasm and practical commentary on its potential market impact.

## 1. Samsung Galaxy Trifold (+ MagSafe Controllers)

A tri-fold smartphone with pocket-sized portability expands for movies, work, and gaming. The bonus feature includes Nintendo-style MagSafe controllers that attach to various phones, particularly iPhones.

## 2. Robo Rock Soros Rover

An advanced robot vacuum featuring stair-climbing capabilities via extendable legs. Launching in 2026, this device represents significant innovation. The author notes that "Robo Rock has really stepped up into the marketplace" as a category leader following competitor challenges.

## 3. Lenovo Legion Pro Rollable

Laptops featuring extendable screens using flat LED technology that rolls from the hinge. While visually impressive for presentations, the author cautions that resolution may disappoint graphics professionals despite the "wow factor."

## 4. Celtics iPhone Rear Screen

A rear-mounted display accessory offering two terabytes of additional storage. The author highlights practical value for creative professionals managing large files.

## 5. Creaseless Foldable Display Innovation

Samsung's advancement in reducing visible creases on foldable devices, including foldable iPhone technology. Multiple manufacturers improved crease transparency significantly this year.

## Bonus: Dell 52-Inch 6K Wraparound Display

A premium ultra-wide display priced competitively against comparable alternatives, anticipated for content creator adoption.

**Conclusion:** The author invites reader engagement on which innovations excite them most, positioning these technologies as industry-defining products for 2026.
