# Top 5 Social Media Trends to Watch Out for in 2022

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/Top-5-Social-Media-Trends-to-Watch-Out-for-in-1  
**Date:** January 2022  
**Author:** Erica in Client Services

---

The article presents five emerging social media trends anticipated to shape marketing strategies in 2022:

## Key Trends

1. **Instagram Reels** will become instrumental in optimizing algorithmic performance for brands
2. **TikTok** offers ecommerce growth opportunities through product showcases and behind-the-scenes content
3. **Twitter Spaces** represents a growing live audio content opportunity
4. **Marketing budgets** will shift toward TikTok, Pinterest, and Snapchat platforms
5. **Instagram's new favorite feature** allows followers to customize feed visibility

## Central Message

The author emphasizes that "meaningful connections with their favorite creators or community" should be the primary focus for 2022 social media strategies.

## Closing Notes

The piece promises deeper exploration of these trends throughout the year and invites readers to contact the company's social media specialists at support@wte.net for further guidance.
