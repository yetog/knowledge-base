Eric Garrison opens by reflecting on WTE Solutions' quarter-century journey, emphasizing the company's commitment to curiosity and innovation. He encourages readers to explore the firm's most popular content before autumn arrives.

The post organizes trending articles into three main categories:

## AI

Martin Wescott-Smith's December piece on ChatGPT is highlighted as particularly influential. The featured AI articles include:

- "ChatGPT: How It Will Change Your Website"
- "ChatGPT 4 Review"
- "AI Your Guide to What's Coming"
- "GitHub Copilot X - The Future of Programming Review"
- "AI Gets Practical"

## Software and Web Development

Garrison discusses WTE's diverse client base, ranging from food trucks to healthcare and banking sectors. Top posts in this category are:

- "Blockchain As Pervasive as Cloud Computing"
- "How Elon's TruthGPT Singularity Impacts Your Future"

- "Using Tech To Track Your Kids Back to School"
