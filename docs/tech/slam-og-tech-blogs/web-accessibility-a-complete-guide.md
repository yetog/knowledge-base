## How Website Accessibility Protects Your Company, Brands & Reputation

The CDC reports that approximately one in four Americans—86 million people—have disabilities and use the internet. Inaccessible websites frustrate these users, damaging customer relationships and business reputation. The author emphasizes that frustrated customers rarely make purchases or become advocates.

## What is Website Accessibility?

Website accessibility refers to designing sites that people with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities can use effectively. Key importance areas include:

- **Inclusion:** Ensures equal opportunities for all users regardless of abilities
- **Accessibility Laws:** Compliance with ADA, WCAG standards, and Section 508 regulations is legally mandated; violations risk lawsuits and fines
- **Usability:** Accessibility improvements benefit all visitors through better navigation and design
- **Reach:** Expands market to millions of people with disabilities
- **SEO:** Accessibility practices enhance search engine rankings
- **Ethics:** Digital accessibility represents a civil right and responsibility
