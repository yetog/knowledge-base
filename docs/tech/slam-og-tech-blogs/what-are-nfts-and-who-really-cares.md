# What are NFTs and who really cares?

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/What-are-NFTs-and-who-really-cares  
**Date:** February 2022  
**Author:** Eric Garrison

---

## Introduction

The term NFT has gained popularity recently, but many people don't understand what it means. NFT stands for Non-fungible Token, representing digital assets such as pictures, documents, and artwork, or physical assets stored digitally. The key distinction is that non-fungible assets are unique and cannot be replaced by something equivalent. By contrast, fungible items like dollar bills can be exchanged—four quarters equal one dollar in value.

## Historical Context

NFTs aren't entirely new. The CryptoPunks project initiated the NFT phenomenon in 2017, followed by the game CryptoKitties, which introduced gameplay elements and technological infrastructure. As the market matured, digital art emerged as a primary use case. Most NFTs consist of drawings, music, photos, or autographed images. Notable figures including Tom Brady, Tony Hawk, Tiger Woods, and Simone Biles launched Autograph.io, an agency that authenticates collectibles, digitizes them, and creates Digital Certificates of Authenticity using the Ethereum Blockchain.

The blockchain's distributed nature across hundreds of computers ensures authenticity. Buyers can verify uniqueness—if purchasing "EDITION: 38/100," they own item 38 of exactly 100. Any fraudulent additions would be visible to all blockchain observers.

## How NFTs Work

A standardized framework enables content creators to convert digital art into crypto art. Typically, one person owns each NFT, though fractional ownership models now exist. For instance, songwriters sell song shares with profit distribution agreements. Auction houses increasingly sell NFTs, helping collectors acquire rare art converted to digital format. Celebrity involvement through platforms like Autograph.io accelerates market growth.

## Financial Considerations

Potential bubble concerns merit consideration. The 1637 Tulip Mania demonstrates how speculative frenzy can cause market collapses. While NFTs may represent legitimate technology, investment advice suggests risking only amounts you can afford to lose completely. Additionally, a concentrated group of traders dominates NFT transactions, potentially indicating market manipulation. Despite these cautions, individuals with creative abilities should consider becoming NFT creators.
