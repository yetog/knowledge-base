## Overview

This gift guide addresses three parent categories: plant parents, human parents, and fur parents. The article provides curated product recommendations with prices and retailer links, plus budget-friendly stocking stuffer options.

## Plant Parents

- **Indoor Herb Garden Kit** ($172.46): "An easy way to grow ultra-fresh herbs" via Click and Grow
- **Lomi Smart Kitchen Composter** ($499.99): Recommended for cooking enthusiasts who compost
- **Houseplant Hippie**: One-stop shop offering plant ordering, workshops, and consultations through various platforms

## Parents (Human)

- **Ember Mug²** ($129.95): Smart temperature control; RED edition donates 3% to global health programs
- **Theragun PRO (4th Gen)** ($399): Uses "smart percussive therapy" for muscle massage and relaxation
- **Crayon Fanatics** ($12–$22.95): Personalized, scented, or themed crayons from Etsy

## Fur Parents

- **DOGNESS Auto Feeder** ($169.99): Automatic feeder with HD camera for pet monitoring
- **The Game™** ($55): Interactive pet toy designed to combat boredom
- **West Street Dog** (Raleigh, NC): Local boarding and dog park services ($28/month+)

## Stocking Stuffers & Budget Options

Affordable alternatives organized by parent type, ranging from $14.99 to $99, including plant kits, wireless chargers, pet treats, and pet furniture.
