# WTE Holiday Gift Guide 2022: SELF CARE

**Source:** https://www.wte.net/WTE-Holiday-Gift-Guide-2022-SELF-CARE  
**Date:** December 2022  
**Author:** WTE Team

---

## Overview

A curated selection of self-care gift recommendations, emphasizing the importance of stress management during the hectic holiday season. The guide encourages readers to prioritize personal wellness through mindfulness and pampering.

## Main Product Recommendations

**Intention Candles** ($27.00)  
Peace Light Company offers candles designed to manifest wishes and establish desired atmospheres through intentional lighting practices.

**The Zen Succulent** (Variable pricing)  
Founded by Megan and Margaret Cain in Durham, this local business specializes in plants that purify air while reducing stress through their natural green presence.

**Mantra Handmade Soaps** ($8+)  
The Mantra Soap Company produces artisanal cleansing bars, including charcoal facial varieties, each backed by a satisfaction guarantee.

**Van Gogh Tea Set** ($65.00 / $58.50 for members)  
The Metropolitan Museum collaborates with Harney & Sons to offer an iris-themed set featuring an infusing mug, tea towel, and chamomile blend.

**Theragun Mini** ($119.20)  
Therabody's portable massage device provides deep-tissue therapy for muscle recovery and stress relief without professional services.

**Moravian Cookies** ($9.99)  
Dewey's bakery, operating since 1930 in Winston-Salem, NC, offers traditional ginger spice cookies for indulgent enjoyment.

## Budget-Friendly Alternatives

The guide includes stocking stuffer options ranging from $20.99 to $59.95+, featuring ice rollers, gel floor mats, flower subscriptions, and gift cards.
